# 카드 뽑기 난이도: 쉬움 (초등 4-6학년)

> 이 프롬프트는 **카드 뽑기에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

- Rarity is NOT difficulty. Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary controls how far the card travels from the source note.
- This difficulty controls the school-year level of vocabulary AND of the underlying topic itself — not just the wording.
- WARNING: do not produce 'easy-sounding wording wrapped around a hard topic'. The TOPIC of the card must match the difficulty too.

TARGET: Korean elementary-school student (4th–6th grade, age 10–12).

TOPIC SCOPE: pick prompts a kid actually cares about — friends, classes, games, family, hobbies, daily small choices. Do NOT pick philosophy, epistemology, abstract theory, business strategy, or research methodology, even dressed in cute words.

VOCABULARY: every visible string (card_name, hook, main_question, bridge_steps, write_now, followups) uses only kid Korean. Banned (do not use these or their synonyms): 메타인지, 인식론, 존재론, 패러다임, 메커니즘, 구조, 전제, 본질, 가치, 효율, 최적화, 추상화, 개념, 사유, 담론, 통찰, 함의, 시사점, 유의미, 상관관계, 표상, 상정, 도출, 정합성, MDL, consilience, meta-epistemic, operationalize, hermeneutic, phenomenological.

QUESTION FEEL: each main_question sounds like a question a curious kid would ask out loud. Answerable in 5–10 minutes.

STEPS: every write_now is a small action a 5th grader can finish in one sitting.

ENERGY: warm, playful, curious — like a cool older sibling explaining, not a teacher lecturing. Add fun emoji occasionally if it fits.

Even a Legendary card is a BIG kid question (예: "내가 좋아하는 일이 진짜 평생 좋아할 일인지 어떻게 알아?"), not an expert-sounding question in baby words.

Self-check before returning: would a 5th grader read this aloud and understand it on first try? If no, simplify the IDEA itself — not just the wording.
