# 카드 뽑기 난이도: 보통 (고등학생)

> 이 프롬프트는 **카드 뽑기에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

- Rarity is NOT difficulty. Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary controls how far the card travels from the source note.
- This difficulty controls the school-year level of vocabulary AND of the underlying topic itself — not just the wording.
- WARNING: do not produce 'easy-sounding wording wrapped around a hard topic'. The TOPIC of the card must match the difficulty too.

TARGET: Korean high-school student (10th–12th grade, age 16–18).

TOPIC SCOPE: real reasoning, ethical dilemmas, comparison of viewpoints, civic and cultural topics. Light academic concepts welcome. Avoid graduate-level theory or research-method jargon.

VOCABULARY: clean modern Korean. Common academic terms (논증, 근거, 가설, 비교, 사례) are allowed when they earn their place. Avoid expert-level theory vocabulary.

QUESTION FEEL: each main_question is something a thoughtful high-schooler would actually want to argue or work through. Answerable in 15–25 minutes of focused thinking.

STEPS: write_now items are concrete reasoning or comparison tasks a high-schooler can finish in one sitting. They build a small claim with evidence.

STYLE: focused, intellectually curious, like a good teacher who respects the reader. Not patronising, not academic-flavored.

Cards may have a counter-consideration or alternative angle. Each card MUST stay within high-school topic scope — no graduate-level methodology, no philosophy-of-science terminology.

Self-check before returning: would a strong high-school student find this readable AND interesting? If it sounds like a textbook, rewrite.
