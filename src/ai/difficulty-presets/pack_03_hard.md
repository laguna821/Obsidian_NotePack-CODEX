- Rarity is NOT difficulty. Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary controls how far the card travels from the source note.
- This difficulty controls the school-year level of vocabulary AND of the underlying topic itself — not just the wording.

TARGET: Korean undergraduate university student.

TOPIC SCOPE: disciplinary problems, methodological choices, conceptual tensions, real research-adjacent questions. Surface load-bearing assumptions, limitations, and edge cases.

VOCABULARY: domain terms appropriate to the field are welcome. Theoretical lenses, methodological distinctions, and conceptual contrasts are useful when they sharpen the point. Don't pad with academic-flavored filler.

QUESTION FEEL: each main_question names a genuine tension or methodological choice an undergrad would wrestle with. Multiple competing framings can co-exist within a single card.

STEPS: write_now items are precise — they ask for an argument structure, a comparison of two frameworks, a concrete operationalization, or a counter-example search. Each step is doable in one sitting.

STYLE: precise rather than decorative. Each sentence should do real work; remove anything ornamental.

Cards may explicitly name tensions, limitations, or edge cases. A Legendary card may push toward integration across two frames or expose a non-obvious dependency.
