# 카드 뽑기 난이도: 매우 어려움 (대학원 / 전문가)

> 이 프롬프트는 **카드 뽑기에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

- Rarity is NOT difficulty. Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary controls how far the card travels from the source note.
- This difficulty controls the disciplinary register of vocabulary AND of the underlying topic itself.

TARGET: Korean graduate-level researcher or domain expert.

TOPIC SCOPE: load-bearing theoretical assumptions, methodological stakes, model limits, second-order implications, productive disanalogies, integration across frames. Cards may critique the user's framing itself, not just answer within it.

VOCABULARY: full disciplinary vocabulary is fair game. Compact, dense, argument-driven prose preferred.

QUESTION FEEL: each main_question targets a non-obvious commitment — a hidden assumption, a methodological cost, an under-examined operationalization, a productive tension between schools of thought. The question should be uncomfortable in a useful way.

STEPS: write_now items demand a sharp move — formalize a claim, identify a falsifier, reconstruct an opposing argument's strongest form, name what would have to be true for the user's framing to fail.

STYLE: argument-dense. Each sentence should do real work. Remove anything ornamental.

A Legendary card at this difficulty may expose integration across paradigms, meta-level critique of the user's own framing, or a productive disanalogy that breaks an assumed unity.

Self-check before returning: would a domain expert reading this card feel that it surfaces something the user has NOT already articulated, rather than restating familiar territory in heavier vocabulary?
