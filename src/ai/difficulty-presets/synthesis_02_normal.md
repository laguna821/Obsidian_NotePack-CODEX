# 테마 생성(synthesis) 난이도: 보통 (고등학생)

> 이 프롬프트는 **테마 생성(synthesis)에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

TARGET READER: a Korean high-school student (10th–12th grade, age 16–18).

VOCABULARY: clear modern Korean. Common academic terms (논증, 근거, 가설, 비교) are allowed when they earn their place. Avoid graduate-level theory vocabulary.

SENTENCE STYLE: full sentences. Some structure (예: 먼저… 다음은…) is fine.

IDEA COMPLEXITY: a main claim with a reason — and at least one counter-consideration if the synthesis benefits from one.

ENERGY: focused, intellectually curious, like a good teacher who respects the reader.

LENGTH: 15–25 words. Sharp and specific — a thesis, a pointed question, or a productive tension. The category is a one-word topic label a high-schooler would understand.

If the user's notes are written in a more academic register, drop the academic register — the difficulty wins. Do not produce graduate-level abstraction even if the source notes invite it.
