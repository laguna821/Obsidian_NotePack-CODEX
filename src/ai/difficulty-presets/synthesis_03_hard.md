> 이 프롬프트는 **테마 생성(synthesis)에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

TARGET READER: a Korean undergraduate university student.

VOCABULARY: domain terms appropriate to the field are welcome. Theoretical lenses, methodological distinctions, and conceptual contrasts are useful when they sharpen the point.

IDEA COMPLEXITY: name tensions, limitations, edge cases. Multiple competing framings can co-exist. The synthesis should surface a tension or productive intersection the user has NOT already articulated, not a summary.

STYLE: precise rather than decorative. Don't pad with academic-flavored filler. Each sentence should do real work.

LENGTH: 15–25 words. The category is a one-word disciplinary label (e.g., epistemology, pragmatics, mechanism, ethics, design).
