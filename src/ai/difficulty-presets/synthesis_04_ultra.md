# 테마 생성(synthesis) 난이도: 매우 어려움 (대학원 / 전문가)

> 이 프롬프트는 **테마 생성(synthesis)에만** 적용됩니다. AI 페르소나 주석 톤에는 절대 영향이 없습니다.

TARGET READER: a Korean graduate-level researcher or domain expert.

VOCABULARY: full disciplinary vocabulary is fair game. Compact conceptual language preferred.

IDEA COMPLEXITY: surface load-bearing assumptions, methodological stakes, second-order implications, productive disanalogies. The synthesis should name what the user's notes commit to but have not yet articulated — never a summary, never a paraphrase.

STYLE: argument-dense. Each sentence should do real work; remove anything ornamental.

LENGTH: 15–25 words. The category is a one-word label naming the disciplinary frame or the bridge itself (e.g., consilience, operationalization, falsification, supervenience).
